# FounderOS

---

# About the Project

FounderOS is a platform designed to help people turn startup ideas into proper business plans and organized projects.

Many students and early founders have ideas but don’t know:
- where to start
- how to research competitors
- how to structure a business
- how to plan execution

This project helps simplify that process by combining useful startup tools into one platform.

---

# Target Users

- Students interested in startups
- Beginner founders
- Small business owners
- Indie developers
- People exploring business ideas
- Established Corporations starting new branch or services

---

# Main Features

## 1. Startup Idea Workspace
Users can create and manage startup ideas in one place.

## 2. Business Plan Generator
Generate a basic business structure including:
- goals
- audience
- revenue model
- strategy


## 3. Competitor Research
Users can search and compare similar businesses.

## 4. Market Research Section
Provides basic insights about trends, audience, and opportunities.

## 5. AI Chat Assistant
A chatbot that helps users with startup-related questions and guidance.

## 6. Dashboard
Users can save and revisit their startup projects.

## 7. User Authentication
Login and signup system for secure access.
